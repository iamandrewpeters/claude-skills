# Planning Center sync

tags: planning center, pco, sync, integration, people, services

If your Faithmade site is connected to Planning Center, content syncs automatically in the background — people, groups, events, and service info depending on what was enabled for your site.

- **Edit in Planning Center, not WordPress.** Synced items are overwritten by the next sync; Planning Center is the source of truth.
- **Sync timing:** changes typically appear on the site within the regular background sync window — not instantly. If something hasn't appeared after an hour, contact support.
- **Connecting for the first time / re-authorizing:** this uses a secure Faithmade authorization flow with your Planning Center admin account — ask support to send you the connect link.
- **Something syncing wrong** (duplicates, missing people, stale events): don't delete the WordPress copies — tell support what you're seeing and we'll fix the sync itself.
