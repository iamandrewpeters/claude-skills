# Adding sermons and the podcast feed

tags: sermons, audio, video, podcast, media, upload, series

To add a sermon: wp-admin → **Sermons → Add New**. Give it a title, pick or create a **Series**, set the date and speaker, then either upload an audio/video file or paste a link (YouTube, Vimeo, or a direct MP3/MP4 URL). Publish — it appears in your sermon lists and the built-in media player automatically.

- **Podcast:** every published sermon with audio is added to your site's podcast feed automatically; Apple/Spotify pick it up from the feed URL your site was submitted with. No extra step per sermon.
- **Series artwork:** edit the Series (Sermons → Series) to set its image; it's used in grids and sliders.
- **Sermon not showing on the page?** Pages display sermons via Beaver Builder modules that update automatically — give caches a few minutes, or ask support to flush the site cache.
- **Wrong order?** Sermon lists sort by the sermon date field, not the publish time — check the date on the sermon.
