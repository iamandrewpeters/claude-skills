# Getting started with your Faithmade site

tags: basics, login, wp-admin, editing, beaver builder

Your Faithmade website runs on WordPress with the Beaver Builder page builder. Log in at `yoursite.org/wp-admin` with the credentials from your onboarding email.

- **Edit a page:** Pages → hover the page → "Beaver Builder". Click any text or image to change it, then Publish (top right).
- **Sermons:** Sermons → Add New. Upload audio/video or paste a link; the sermon player and podcast feed update automatically.
- **Groups:** Groups → Add New for small groups, with schedule and signup details.
- **Events:** Events → Add New; they appear in your events lists and calendars automatically.
- **Site colors & fonts:** Appearance → Customize. Changes preview live before you publish.
- **Planning Center:** if your site is connected, people, groups, and events sync automatically — edit those in Planning Center, not WordPress.

If something looks broken after editing, don't panic: ask support to restore the page — every site is backed up.
