# Managing groups and events

tags: groups, small groups, events, calendar, signups, schedule

**Groups:** wp-admin → **Groups → Add New**. Set the name, description, meeting schedule, and signup/contact details. Published groups appear on your groups page automatically, with a signup modal for visitors.

**Events:** wp-admin → **Events → Add New**. Set date/time, location, and description; events appear in your event lists and calendars and drop off automatically after they pass.

**Connected to Planning Center?** If your site syncs with Planning Center, groups and events flow in from there — edit them in Planning Center, not WordPress (WordPress edits to synced items get overwritten on the next sync). Synced items show a Planning Center note on their edit screen.

Common questions:
- *Event didn't show up?* Check it's published and the date is in the future; lists hide past events.
- *Group signup goes to the wrong email?* Edit the group's contact field.
