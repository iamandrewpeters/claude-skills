# Changing your site's colors and fonts

tags: customizer, colors, fonts, branding, styles, appearance

Go to wp-admin → **Appearance → Customize**. Your brand colors and typography live there, and color changes preview instantly before you publish.

- Changes apply site-wide — buttons, links, headings, and section styling all inherit from these settings, so you rarely need to restyle individual pages.
- Some deeper style options trigger a full style rebuild when you publish; give the site a few seconds to reflect them.
- If a specific section looks off after a color change, it may have a per-section override set in Beaver Builder — open the page in the builder and check that row's style settings.
- Logo and favicon are also under Appearance → Customize.

If you want a broader rebrand (new palette, new fonts), reach out — we'll apply it consistently across your whole site.
